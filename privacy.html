Privacy & Safety

We want future members to understand what is and is not active while the platform is being developed.

Current website status

This is an informational static website preview. It does not currently have member accounts, a registration database, matching, private messaging or payment processing. The founding-member form is a demonstration and does not save or transmit responses.

Protect your information

  • Do not submit passwords, card details, bank information, identity documents or precise home addresses.
  • Do not send money to someone you have only met online without independently verifying the situation.
  • Keep early conversations on trusted channels and respect personal boundaries.
  • For future in-person meetings, choose public places and tell someone you trust about your plans.

Before real registration opens

We plan to select a secure form and data-storage provider, publish a complete privacy notice, explain how information will be used and retained, and establish moderation and reporting processes. Any future service will need appropriate legal and safety review for the countries it serves.

This page is an initial informational notice, not legal advice or a complete privacy policy.
