Privacy & Safety

Your Past Is a Chapter. Your Future Is Still a Story.

FOREVER BEGINS AGAIN is developing a global community for adults interested in meaningful love and companionship, friendship, business networking, leisure, travel, retreats and exploring new possibilities together.

Current website status: This is an early-stage website. Member accounts, matching, private messaging and payment processing are not currently available. Founding-member registration is collected through a separate Google Form, with responses linked to Google Sheets.

1. Information we collect

The founding-member form may collect your name, email address, country of residence, confirmation that you are at least 18 years old, community interests, preferred experiences and choices about receiving launch updates. It may also record your response to the privacy acknowledgement.

2. How we use your information

Information is used to manage founding-member enquiries, understand interest in the proposed community, plan the launch and, where you have opted in, send relevant launch updates. We do not promise membership, matching or access to features that have not yet been launched.

3. Form and data storage

Registration responses are collected using Google Forms and linked to Google Sheets. Access to the response spreadsheet should be restricted to authorised people who need the information to manage registrations. The form and spreadsheet are subject to the relevant Google services' terms and privacy practices.

4. Sharing and publication

We do not intend to publish your individual registration details on the website or share them publicly. Information should only be accessed or disclosed for legitimate registration purposes, with your permission where required, or where disclosure is legally required. Do not assume that information submitted online can be kept absolutely secure.

5. Launch updates

We will use your email address for launch updates only if you choose to receive them. You should be able to ask us to stop sending these updates. An update request does not automatically delete your registration record.

6. Access, correction and deletion requests

You may contact the FOREVER BEGINS AGAIN team to ask about your registration information or request its correction or deletion. We will review such requests and respond subject to applicable law and any legitimate record-retention requirements.

7. Information you should not submit

Please do not submit passwords, bank or card details, identity documents, precise home addresses, sensitive medical information or other unnecessary confidential information through the founding-member form.

8. Online and personal safety

  • Never send money to someone solely because you met them online.
  • Protect your passwords and financial information.
  • Respect personal boundaries and report suspicious behaviour when reporting facilities become available.
  • If meeting someone in person, choose a public location and tell someone you trust about your plans.

9. International users and future features

FOREVER BEGINS AGAIN intends to serve adults in different countries. Privacy requirements can vary by location. Before launching full membership, matching, messaging or payment features, we will need to review applicable legal requirements and establish suitable privacy, security, moderation and reporting arrangements.

10. Contact and questions

For privacy questions, correction requests or deletion requests, please use the contact method provided on our Contact Us page. Before inviting the public to register, the team should ensure that a monitored contact email address is available.

Important: This page provides general privacy information and is not legal advice. It should be reviewed for the applicable laws and the actual data-handling arrangements before a wider public launch.
